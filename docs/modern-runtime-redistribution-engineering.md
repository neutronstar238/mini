## Redistribution boundary

The manifest and evidence both record the following fixed boundary:

- `TECHNICAL_VALIDATED=true`
- `ENGINEERING_REDISTRIBUTION_READY=true`
- `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED=true`
- `REDISTRIBUTABLE=false`

Engineering readiness confirms the overlay's identity, asset references,
integrity metadata, deterministic generator output, and no-copy layout. It does
not grant redistribution permission. Legal review remains required, and the
runtime must not be presented as redistributable until that review changes the
recorded decision.
